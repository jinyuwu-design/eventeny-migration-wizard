Set up payout method

Eventeny uses Stripe to release payouts to you. We do not hold any bank account information in our system. The time it takes for the funds to appear in your account depends on the payment and payout methods. For additional information, visit Payout and Payment Method.

Set up payout method through Stripe (10 mins)
  1. You'll be directed to create a Stripe account (our third-party payment processing software). You must create a Stripe account in order to receive payments on Eventeny. Go ahead and go through the prompts to get set up with Stripe.
  2. When you are finished creating a Stripe account, you'll be redirected back to the Eventeny's company details page which will show your newly created payout method. The button in the middle should be green and read, "Enabled." If you have any questions, please contact Eventeny customer support.
